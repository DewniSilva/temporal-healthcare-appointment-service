Temporal Healthcare Appointment Service — data model

Ten tables, three lifecycles, one clinic.

Every table behind booking, confirmation, reminders, and per-doctor scheduling — read top to bottom as one chart, or scan the reference cards below the diagram for exact field types. appointmentTime and every timestamp are UTC; wall-clock fields like startTime stay clinic-local on purpose.
